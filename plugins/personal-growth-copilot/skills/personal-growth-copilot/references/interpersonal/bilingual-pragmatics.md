# Bilingual and Mixed-Language Workplace Pragmatics

Use this reference for English-Simplified Chinese adaptation, mixed-language
workplace cases, or channel choices involving email, chat, WeChat, documents,
and meetings.

## Preserve function, not sentence shape

Draft each language independently for the same interpersonal function. Preserve:

- facts, source limits, and uncertainty;
- primary recommendation and decision right;
- obligation and meaningful ability to decline;
- boundary and user-controlled consequence;
- escalation and stopping conditions;
- authority, speaker identity, and intended audience;
- user agency.

Permitted variation includes address terms, sentence order, amount of context,
relational buffering, directness, and channel. Variation must not weaken or
intensify the substantive act without the user's informed choice.

## Classify the speech act first

Identify the leading act before drafting:

- **request:** asks another person to do or decide something;
- **refusal:** declines a request or condition;
- **disagreement:** challenges a position, criterion, assumption, or decision;
- **attribution correction:** changes a public or durable record of contribution;
- **feedback/accountability:** seeks learning or requires behavior change;
- **apology/repair:** acknowledges supported harm and proposes remedy;
- **escalation:** moves a concern to a person or process with greater authority;
- **reminder/follow-up:** seeks performance of an existing commitment;
- **invitation/networking ask:** requests time, access, introduction, or support.

A message may contain more than one act, but keep one primary act visible.

## Calibrate with variables, not stereotypes

Consider:

- speaker and receiver roles, authority, and dependency;
- relationship history and prior communication;
- public versus private setting;
- imposition, urgency, and consequence of misunderstanding;
- need for a durable record;
- shared language proficiency and processing time;
- local team and institutional norms;
- mixed-language audience and likely forwarding;
- accessibility needs and channel constraints.

Treat hierarchy, face, indirectness, and directness as questions about the local
interaction, not national facts about a person. Never infer a private motive or
preferred style from nationality, ethnicity, age, or gender.

## Use the speech-act adapter

For each language version identify:

1. the act and intended outcome;
2. the observable context;
3. the degree of imposition and the receiver's actual discretion;
4. the authority or decision right;
5. the minimum relational framing needed for reception;
6. the substantive request, refusal, correction, or remedy;
7. owner, date, and record where material;
8. escalation or stop condition.

Then adapt register and sequence without changing the substance.

### Request upward

English:

> To protect Friday's release, I need a priority decision today: should I finish
> A, or move the team to B and shift A to Monday? Both are not feasible with the
> current verification requirement.

Simplified Chinese:

> 为了确保周五交付，我需要今天确认优先级：是先完成 A，还是把团队转到 B，并把 A
> 调整到周一？在保留现有验证要求的情况下，两项无法同时完成。

The Chinese version adds context but does not hide the decision request or
accept incompatible work.

### Firm refusal

English:

> I can't take ownership of this additional task. The current owner will need to
> remain accountable, or the decision owner must reassign it explicitly.

Simplified Chinese:

> 这项新增任务我无法接手负责。现有负责人需要继续承担责任；如需调整，请由有决策权的人
> 明确重新分配。

Do not convert a refusal into “I will try” or “perhaps it may be difficult.”

### Attribution correction

English:

> The analysis was described as Alex's work. I built the model, and Alex
> incorporated it into the deck. Please correct the recap to name both
> contributions.

Simplified Chinese:

> 会议中这项分析被表述为 Alex 的成果。模型由我建立，Alex 将其整合进了演示文稿。
> 请在会议纪要中更正，并准确注明双方的贡献。

Do not make the English version accusatory or the Chinese version submissive.

### Disagreement under hierarchy

English:

> I understand the direction. Before implementation, I need the safety exception
> recorded and confirmed by the design authority. My recommendation remains A
> because ___.

Simplified Chinese:

> 我理解当前决定。在执行前，我需要由设计权责方确认并记录这项安全例外。基于 ___，
> 我的建议仍然是方案 A。

Respectful framing does not erase the professional recommendation or required
record.

### Apology and repair

English:

> I sent the wrong attachment and exposed the draft. That was my error. I have
> removed access, notified the authorized owner, and added a two-person release
> check.

Simplified Chinese:

> 我发送了错误的附件，导致内部草稿被不当暴露。这是我的失误。我已经撤回访问权限、
> 通知了有权处理此事的负责人，并增加了双人发布复核。

Do not fabricate emotion, promise the breach will never recur, or ask for
immediate forgiveness.

## Mixed-language handling

When input or audience is mixed:

- preserve who said which words and in which language;
- quote only the excerpts needed for the decision;
- identify terms whose force or authority may not transfer literally;
- draft for the actual recipient rather than mirror the source language;
- use parallel versions only when both will be sent or reviewed;
- avoid switching languages to conceal a stronger threat, weaker refusal, or
  unsupported certainty.

If a durable English record follows a Chinese conversation, state decisions,
owners, dates, unresolved items, and the correction window rather than claim
that every nuance was agreed.

## Channel patterns

- Use chat or WeChat for narrow coordination and quick clarification.
- Use email or another durable channel when ownership, deadlines, attribution,
  authority, or formal record matters.
- Use a meeting for negotiation or repair, then confirm the result in writing.
- Prefer asynchronous material before a meeting when language processing,
  accessibility, or power makes live response difficult.
- Do not place unnecessary third-party identifiers or confidential material
  into an AI draft or group channel.

## Parity check

For every consequential pair verify:

1. same facts and source limits;
2. same unresolved unknowns and confidence;
3. same recommendation and decision right;
4. same owner, deadline, and dependency;
5. same meaningful ability to decline;
6. same boundary and consequence;
7. same escalation trigger;
8. same stopping condition;
9. same authority and representational scope;
10. natural register for the actual relationship and forum.

Items 1-9 are hard invariants. Item 10 requires fluent human judgment for
material use.

## Failure modes

- literal translation that changes practical force;
- deferential Chinese wording that silently accepts incompatible commitments;
- direct English wording that adds accusation or dominance;
- nationality-based personality claims;
- role-incongruent forms that expose a lower-power speaker unnecessarily;
- omission of owner, deadline, boundary, or record because they sound impolite;
- code-switching that conceals coercion, certainty, or an unsupported authority
  claim;
- claiming native-level calibration without fluent review.

## Review requirement

For consequential bilingual messages, recommend review by a fluent speaker who
knows the relationship, institution, and local workplace context. Synthetic
parity checks can establish functional consistency; they cannot establish
native naturalness or status calibration.

## Evidence boundary

Cross-cultural and second-language pragmatics supports analyzing speech acts,
context, proficiency, and situational variation. It does not supply national
scripts or person-level predictions. See `CULTURE-01`, `PRAGMATICS-02`, and
`PRAGMATICS-03` in the evidence ledger.
