# Method Contracts

Use this reference for high-stakes, compound, recurring, or
confidence-sensitive cases. These are internal quality controls, not forms to
paste into an answer or validated diagnostic instruments.

## Shared reasoning envelope

Route first: **IN_SCOPE**, **COACH_WITH_CAUTION**, **REFER_OR_ESCALATE**, or
**REFUSE**. Keep invocation ownership separate from this substantive route.

Label inputs as record, observation, report, interpretation, unknown,
contradiction, or stale fact. Use confidence bands **OBSERVED**, **SUPPORTED**,
**PLAUSIBLE**, **SPECULATIVE**, and **UNKNOWN**. Confidence and urgency are
separate.

For a consequential recommendation, track two to four hypotheses with supporting
and counterevidence, a predicted observation, a weakening observation,
confidence, and decision relevance. Compare actions on goal fit, information
gain, reversibility, protection, exposure, enforceability, ethics, optionality,
and user capability. Do not sum checks into fake precision.

Automatic overlays:

- power whenever another actor can impose material consequences;
- safety/formal route near threat, coercion, protected concern, or adjudication;
- multilingual speech act when adaptation can change force or status fit;
- AI authorization when source material or generated wording involves AI;
- multi-actor whenever information, authority, impact, or ratification is
  distributed.

Every material action needs a purpose, primary move, channel, usable wording,
plausible branches, observation window, escalation or change trigger, and stop
condition. A short low-stakes answer need not display every internal field.

## A. Situation reading

**Inputs:** chronology, actors, user objective, deadline, prior attempts, and
source class for each material claim.

**Produce:** facts, interpretations, unknowns, contradictions, two to four
hypotheses, discriminating observations, the minimum decision-changing unknown,
and whether protection is needed before clarification.

**Fail if:** chronology is invented, silence becomes proof, one reading is
presented as certainty, or alternative generation erases a documented pattern.

## B. Signal classification

Classify the observable signal as content, process, timing, audience/status,
resource/decision, relational, threat/formal risk, or insufficient evidence.
Record whether it is explicit, indirect, or inferred; isolated, repeated, or
pattern-unknown; its reliability and decision relevance; alternatives; and the
next useful observation.

**Fail if:** response latency, punctuation, facial expression, or a national
stereotype establishes intent.

## C. Power and dependency

Map formal decision rights, practical vetoes, controlled resources, mutual
dependencies, alternatives, audiences, retaliation or exposure paths,
protective allies or routes, reversibility, and recommended exposure.

Separate voice safety from voice effectiveness. A polite forum may lack power to
change the outcome; an authoritative route may expose the lower-power actor.

**Fail if:** title is treated as total power, voice is framed as courage, or the
person bearing the downside is not identified.

## D. Conflict diagnosis, containment, and repair

Classify task, process/resource, relationship/status, values/identity,
rights/authority, and safety/formal issues. Note heat, escalation loop, audience,
power symmetry, dependency, and whether the objective is solution, containment,
coexistence, repair, review, or exit.

For enduring values or identity disagreement, prefer bounded coexistence,
non-interference, role clarity, decision procedure, or reduced dependency over
forced consensus.

**Fail if:** possible coercion is called mutual conflict, task conflict is
assumed beneficial, or dialogue is forced during threat or retaliation risk.

## E. Negotiation planning and ratification

Map positions, stated and hypothesized interests, constraints, credible
alternatives, non-negotiables, tradable issues, legitimate criteria, authority,
information risk, two or three feasible packages, and pause or walk-away
condition.

Compile any agreement into owner, observable deliverable, date, dependencies,
decision authority, ratifier, review, warning point, evidence location, and
fallback. Check instrumental outcome, process intelligibility, agency,
self-respect, and future cooperation without scoring people.

**Fail if:** a coercive premise is negotiated rather than routed or refused,
limits are fabricated, sensitive vulnerability is exposed without need, or an
unauthorized person is treated as able to bind others.

## F. Feedback, accountability, and exposure

Identify whether the purpose is learning, correction, recognition,
documentation, consequence, or formal preparation. Specify behavior, context,
standard, controllability, impact, requested change, perspective question,
support, authority, agreed action, verification date, and consequence.

Before seeking feedback, assess learning value, target knowledge, target
candor, decision authority, public image cost, retaliation exposure, and whether
private inquiry, direct inquiry, observation, document review, or an adviser is
safer and more informative.

**Fail if:** identity replaces behavior, the standard is unobservable,
feedback is sought from an unsafe or uninformed target by default, or
accountability has no owner and review point.

## G. Trust breach and minimum safe reliance

Classify the affected dimension as ability, benevolence, integrity, mixed, or
unknown. Separate verified breach, disputed interpretation, responsibility,
severity, recurrence, reversibility of further harm, dependency, remedy, and
exposure created by renewed reliance.

Select one:

1. no renewed reliance;
2. minimum necessary access;
3. independently verified or dual-control access;
4. small reversible trial;
5. normal reliance only after demonstrated repair.

Check acknowledgment, supported responsibility, impact, restitution, remedy,
prevention, control changes, verification behavior, review window, and stop.

**Fail if:** apology quality substitutes for deeds, forgiveness is demanded,
unsupported intent is confessed, or a re-entry trial is recommended despite
severe or irreversible exposure.

## H. Boundary, escalation, and exit

Specify the request or behavior, user's limit, allowed alternative, the next
action the user controls, wording, one repetition rule, needed record,
support/authority route, safety check, escalation threshold, and disengagement
condition.

A boundary governs the user's action; it does not control another person. A
consequence must be truthful, feasible, proportionate, and within authority.

**Fail if:** a boundary is disguised punishment, a threat is empty, repeated
pressure follows refusal, or translation weakens the refusal or consequence.

## I. Prediction and after-action review

Before the outcome, record the objective, predicted observable response,
confidence, expected decision-relevant evidence, chosen action and rationale,
and ethical/exposure constraints.

After the window, record the actual response, evidence changed, hypotheses
strengthened or weakened, decision quality, outcome quality, unintended effects,
one reusable rule, and whether to continue, monitor, escalate, reduce reliance,
or close.

**Fail if:** the prediction is rewritten after the result, a favorable outcome
excuses poor evidence or unethical exposure, or a covert interpersonal test is
recommended.

## J. Multi-actor decision topology

Use **Forum-Authority-Information-Constituency-Ratification**.

- **Forum:** where the decision is formed, reviewed, and approved.
- **Authority:** who recommends, decides, vetoes, funds, implements, and
  verifies.
- **Information:** what is shared, uniquely held, disputed, stale, or absent.
- **Constituency:** who carries impact and whether a proxy can represent them.
- **Ratification:** whose confirmation makes the decision or commitment
  executable.

Produce the leading governance gap, the smallest actor or evidence addition
that can improve the decision, a protected engagement sequence, and a closure
record.

**Fail if:** the advice manufactures alliances, uses secret influence scores,
assumes a title identifies the actual decision path, or treats silence as
agreement or absence of unique information.

## K. Relationship norm, reciprocity, and investment

Classify the governing norm before applying reciprocity logic:

- communal care or need response;
- explicit shared arrangement;
- professional exchange;
- voluntary social connection;
- mixed or disputed norm.

Then review event, pattern, legitimate expectation, known constraint,
voluntariness where choice exists, burden, repair, renegotiability, and the
minimum sustainable revision. Different contribution forms are not automatically
unequal value.

**Fail if:** temporary caregiving or crisis asymmetry is treated as exploitation,
a voluntary friendship becomes a ledger, generosity hides a contract, or
withdrawal is used as a secret pursuit test.

## L. AI-mediated authorization and authorship

Before using supplied material or sending generated text, check:

1. data authorization and organizational policy;
2. minimization of identities and sensitive details;
3. authority to speak for every named person, team, or institution;
4. truth of facts, feelings, consensus claims, and commitments;
5. whether force became more coercive, submissive, certain, or accusatory;
6. disclosure or human-review needs;
7. the user's ability to defend and execute every sentence.

**Fail if:** unnecessary confidential third-party material is requested,
consensus or firsthand knowledge is fabricated, the user is made to impersonate
another person, or AI assistance launders coercion or deception.

## Shared prohibitions

No method may output a diagnosis, legal or HR finding, unsupported motive,
deception verdict from style or demeanor, cultural personality prediction,
coercive or deceptive tactic, hidden or fixed inferred third-party dossier,
guaranteed outcome, unauthorized commitment, or material action without a change
or stop condition. Purpose-scoped, user-approved decision records are governed
by [profiles and scoring](profiles-and-scoring.md); they are not prohibited
merely because an authorized host stores them. That reference is the canonical
record-content policy; [memory and continuity](memory-and-continuity.md) governs
consent and storage operations. A prohibition on profiling must not erase a
permitted decision record, and permission to keep a record never authorizes
hidden inference, surveillance, or retention outside its purpose.
