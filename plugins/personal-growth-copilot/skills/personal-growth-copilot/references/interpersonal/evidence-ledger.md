# Interpersonal evidence namespace

The source IDs below identify the interpersonal lineage. They are not automatically valid citation IDs in the growth-record schema. Keep those namespaces distinct; a rejected citation must not be fabricated or silently remapped. Source-specific summaries support bounded reasoning, not consolidated efficacy claims.

# Evidence and Claim Ledger

This ledger constrains runtime research claims. It is a compact projection of
`provenance/evidence-sources.json`, which is the release-governed source of
truth. Research supports general mechanisms and contraindications; it does not
establish a specific person's motive, a legal or clinical category, or a
guaranteed intervention outcome.

## Use rules

- State only the permitted claim and apply the linked runtime rule.
- Carry the limitation into any material generalization.
- Treat cross-cultural findings as context variables, never person-level
  predictions.
- Treat conceptual and practitioner frameworks as scaffolds, not causal proof.
- Verify current official and jurisdiction-specific guidance before a formal
  claim.
- When population, language, authority, or setting differs materially from the
  user's case, narrow the claim.

## Practitioner-framework quarantine

The following branded frameworks may supply memorable prompts only. Do not
describe them as validated causal interventions, copy proprietary prose, or let
a mnemonic override power, safety, evidence, or stopping conditions.

| Framework | Permitted role | Quarantine rule |
|---|---|---|
| Situation-Behavior-Impact | Prompt for behavior-specific feedback | Add standard, controllability, support, commitment, review, and power context. |
| Nonviolent Communication | Prompt to separate observation, need, and request | Do not require branded vocabulary or use it where protection or authority is needed. |
| Crucial Conversations | Practitioner prompts for difficult dialogue | Source underlying mechanisms separately; do not imply independent validation. |
| Radical Candor | Reminder to combine directness with regard | Do not excuse dominance, harshness, or personality judgment. |
| Difficult Conversations | Prompts for contribution, intent, and identity | Do not manufacture mutual contribution or false symmetry. |
| Getting to Yes | Negotiation scaffold | Use interests, alternatives, criteria, and packages without presenting the brand as empirical proof. |
| Thomas-Kilmann modes | Vocabulary for response choices | Do not type people or choose a mode without power and safety analysis. |
| DESC | Prompt for description, request, and consequence | Keep consequences truthful and user-controlled; do not promise behavior change. |

## Original product safeguards

The classification tables, Power-Reversibility-Exposure scan, relationship-norm
gate, minimum-safe-reliance gate, AI-authorization gate, commitment compilation,
and stopping rules are original product safeguards synthesized from the promoted
mechanisms. They are not validated diagnostic instruments and must not be
presented as scientific scales.

## Review triggers

Review or demote a claim when:

- its DOI, PMID, official-document identity, author, or title no longer matches
  the source registry;
- a newer systematic review materially changes the permitted claim;
- runtime wording becomes stronger than the recorded claim;
- a cultural finding is used as an individual prediction;
- an official route or jurisdiction-specific claim is added without current
  verification;
- behavior tests show overconfidence, false symmetry, unsafe voice advice,
  over-contracting of communal relationships, or individualization of
  structural harm.
