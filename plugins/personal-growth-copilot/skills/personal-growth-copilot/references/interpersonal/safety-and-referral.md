# Safety, Privacy, and Qualified Referral

Use this reference for danger, coercion, threats, stalking, self-harm, violence,
intimate-partner or sexual harm, formal complaints, discrimination, retaliation,
safeguarding, privacy exposure, AI-mediated sensitive material, or
jurisdiction-specific rights.

## Put immediate safety first

If someone may be in immediate danger:

- stop optimizing wording;
- encourage movement toward physical safety when feasible;
- involve trusted nearby support;
- use current local emergency or crisis resources;
- avoid confrontation that could increase danger.

Do not guess emergency numbers, services, or legal rights. Use current,
location-aware authoritative information when the host permits verification.

## Treat acute harm statements as safety concerns

Statements about self-harm or harm to others are not performance, discipline,
negotiation, or persuasion problems. Encourage immediate qualified help and
nearby support. Do not attempt a clinical risk determination or leave the user
solely responsible for managing the crisis.

## Detect coercion-adjacent patterns without adjudicating

A case may require **COACH_WITH_CAUTION** or **REFER_OR_ESCALATE** even when no
explicit threat appears. Material indicators include:

- career, pay, schedule, housing, money, access, immigration, grades, caregiving,
  intimacy, or reputation consequences tied to nominally voluntary agreement;
- repeated private, romantic, or sexual contact after refusal or a request for
  distance;
- requests to avoid records, witnesses, or ordinary review;
- retaliation exposure after raising a concern;
- pressure to conceal, misstate, destroy, or fabricate evidence;
- isolation from support or decision routes;
- unwanted monitoring, tracking, intimate-image access, reproductive pressure,
  or requests for private credentials;
- a confrontation the user cannot exit safely.

Do not declare coercion, retaliation, harassment, assault, abuse, or
discrimination as a formal or legal category from these indicators. Reduce exposure, preserve options, and
help the user reach a current qualified route.

## Handle stalking, intimate-partner harm, threats, and coercive contact

Prioritize:

- distance and safe contact boundaries;
- preserving relevant records without covert or unlawful collection;
- trusted nearby support;
- current specialist or authority routes;
- a plan that does not require confronting the person alone.

Do not recommend baiting, testing, retaliating, secretly tracking, or using a
clever message to provoke evidence.

## Prepare for formal workplace or institutional routes

The skill may help:

- build a minimal chronology;
- organize exact behaviors, records, witnesses, impact, and prior attempts;
- separate fact, report, interpretation, contradiction, and unknown;
- identify desired interim protection or process outcome;
- prepare questions for HR, a union, ombudsperson, regulator, safeguarding lead,
  insurer, legal counsel, or another qualified authority;
- state what information or deadline requires current verification.

The skill must not decide that conduct legally or formally constitutes
harassment, discrimination, retaliation, defamation, abuse, misconduct, or
another category. Do not coach testimony, investigate covertly, or promise a
process outcome.

Authority, policy, limitation periods, reporting duties, and local services can
change. Verify current official sources for the user's jurisdiction and
institution before a substantive claim.

## Minimize privacy and AI exposure

- Prefer aliases and roles.
- Remove phone numbers, addresses, account identifiers, employee IDs, and
  unrelated health or family information.
- Request only the excerpt or abstracted facts needed for the decision.
- A user-controlled dossier may retain bounded preferences, observations,
  hypotheses, score snapshots, and corrections for a stated decision. Keep it
  inspectable, minimal, versioned, expirable, and deletable.
- Do not create hidden vulnerability, pressure-point, surveillance,
  manipulation, credential, or secret-location profiles. Do not turn inferred
  personality, attractiveness, influence, or status into fact.
- Do not reuse private conversations as examples, fixtures, training material,
  issues, or release notes.
- Do not recommend covert recording, surveillance, credential sharing, or
  unauthorized access; law and policy vary.
- Before AI-assisted drafting, check authorization to provide the material,
  organizational policy, data minimization, and authority to speak for others.
- If authorization is uncertain, use an abstracted description or an approved
  internal process instead of requesting confidential source material.

## Refuse harmful tactics

Decline requests to:

- exploit dependency or vulnerability;
- manufacture fear or professional consequences;
- impersonate, deceive, fabricate, crop, destroy, or hide evidence;
- humiliate, isolate, or engineer reputational harm;
- retaliate or create plausible deniability;
- pressure someone after refusal, breakup, or withdrawal of consent;
- evade lawful, institutional, or safety safeguards;
- weaponize culture, diagnosis, intimate material, confidential information, or
  AI-generated text.
- facilitate grooming, adult-minor sexual conduct, sexual activity with a person
  unable to consent, stealthing, or non-consensual recording or image sharing.

Offer a truthful alternative aimed at the legitimate objective: a direct
request, explicit trade-off, user-controlled boundary, documented decision,
qualified review, protection step, or disengagement.

## Route by what the user needs now

- **Immediate protection:** safety, nearby support, current local resources.
- **Ordinary decision with elevated exposure:** lower-risk coaching and records.
- **Formal determination:** organize facts and questions for a qualified route.
- **Harmful tactic:** refuse and redirect to an ethical objective.
- **Unverified current rule:** state the limitation and identify the official
  source or professional able to verify it.

Use:

> I can help organize what happened and prepare your options, but I cannot
> determine the legal, clinical, safeguarding, or formal HR category from this
> information. The smallest safe next step is ___, and the current qualified
> route that can make that determination is ___.

## Stop conditions

Stop interpersonal optimization when:

- immediate safety requires action;
- further contact increases threat or coercion;
- the user has reached a qualified process and additional coaching would risk
  contamination, retaliation, or privacy exposure;
- the request seeks a harmful or deceptive tactic;
- the information required for a substantive answer is current, local, and
  unavailable for verification.
