# Leadership sources and boundaries

All twelve methods retain job, prerequisites, output and stop/switch rules. Programme ideas include purpose/practical/participation contracting; transition from personal execution to enabling others; experience/review/conclusion/next attempt; feedback as a difference between intent and impact; and keeping authority, competence, capacity and free participation separate. SCARF and other programme models are optional self-inquiry prompts, never another person's diagnosis. Role-transition maps do not establish promotion or a changed job.

The source library's book additions include Marquet (control/competence/clarity), Bungay Stanier (helpful questions and bounded support), Rumelt (diagnosis, guiding policy, coherent actions), Wiseman (2010 and revised 2017 editions kept distinct), Stone/Patton/Heen (*Difficult Conversations*, 2023), Stone/Heen (*Thanks for the Feedback*, 2014), Edmondson (*The Fearless Organization*, catalog 2018/copyright 2019), Schein (*Humble Inquiry*, third edition 2025), Heifetz and colleagues (technical/adaptive work), Grove (one-to-ones), Kegan/Lahey (*Immunity to Change*, 2009), Weinberg (*Becoming a Technical Leader*, internal 2014/catalog 2010), Muller (*Systems Architecting*, 2011), Maier/Rechtin (*The Art of Systems Architecting*, fourth edition 2025), and Larson (*Staff Engineer*, 2021/internal copyright 2020). The portable cards/guides are editorial adaptations from inspected source-library syntheses, not fresh original-book verification or reproductions.

For a source quotation, verify the specified original passage when available; otherwise state its absence. Original source locators are optional context, not runtime dependencies. No full books, personal assessments, participant transcripts, colleague plans or named private workplace records are distributed. The updated 2023 Ibarra edition remains unavailable in the inspected source shelf; do not claim it was integrated. ETC's missing procedure and uncertain programme diagrams are not completed by inference.

The [practice guides](leadership-guides.md) retain development, communication, change, pressure, technical contribution and review routes. Clinical, legal, personnel-appraisal, formal qualification and controlled operational decisions retain their proper owners. A method match or a completed course cannot certify capability or effectiveness.
