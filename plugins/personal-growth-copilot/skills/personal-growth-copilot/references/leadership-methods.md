# Leadership methods: portable practice cards

These concise adaptations retain the canonical L01–L12 identifiers. Read the relevant
card after [situation matching](leadership-practice.md#start-with-the-users-job), not all
cards as a compulsory session script. Source lineage and local adaptation limits are
in [leadership-programme.md](leadership-programme.md#method-lineage). The openings below
are suggested wording, not quotations from programme participants.

## L01 — Delegate with boundaries and support

**Opening:** “What could you own, and what decisions and support would make that workable?”
Check outcome, task knowledge, capacity, and authority with the prospective owner.
Agree independent decisions, approvals, acceptance evidence, help, escalation, and a
checkpoint. Clarify what the leader stops doing and what accountability remains.
**Observe:** explainable judgments, quality, available support, hidden rework; fewer
questions alone is not success. **Switch:** teach/pair for a knowledge gap; L09 for
capacity; L02 for options. Never delegate authority you lack or remove required checks.

## L02 — GROW coaching

**Opening:** “Would coaching, advice, or a decision help most now?”
Clarify Goal, examine Reality through a concrete example, explore Options, then the
person chooses a Will/Way Forward: action, support, and review. Move between these as
needed, rather than enforcing a rigid sequence. **Observe:** a feasible choice owned
by the person. **Switch:** instruction when knowledge is missing; direct explanation
for a fixed decision. Do not disguise a performance warning as open coaching.

## L03 — Situation, behavior, impact, then intent inquiry

**Opening:** “In that review, I noticed ___. The effect I observed was ___. What was happening from your side?”
Use a specific event and observed behavior; separate inferred impact from observation.
Hear context and intent, correct your account or pause to check a dispute. Agree a
behavior/support change, or retain disagreement and the appropriate next route.
**Observe:** a clearer shared account or explicit dispute, not just agreement.
**Stop:** sensitive allegations need the appropriate protected process. Silence is not
consent; the leader's interpretation is also open to correction.

## L04 — Negotiate interests and tradeoffs

**Opening:** “What result and constraint does each position protect?”
Separate interests from positions; identify alternatives if there is no agreement.
Agree relevant criteria, compare credible packages including burdens on absent parties,
and record authorized terms, owner, and review, or no agreement. **Observe:** whether
commitments can actually be honored. **Stop:** do not bargain away protections or
commit another owner's resources. Coercion or retaliation may require a mediated or
protected route rather than a direct negotiation.

## L05 — 1-2-4-All participation

**Opening:** “This is the question, who will decide, and what your input can change.”
Allow individual thinking, then pairs, groups of four, and whole-group contributions.
Provide accessible or separate input options. Retain objections and explain what input
changed or why it was not adopted. **Observe:** useful contributions and decision
traceability, not headcount alone. **Stop/switch:** unsafe visible disagreement calls
for another route; a known plan may need L06. Do not delay urgent required action or
promise authority participants do not have.

## L06 — Brief or huddle on a shared plan

**Opening:** “What changed, what stays, and what does that mean for your next action?”
Name the goal, decision owner, evidence, roles, constraints, and unknowns. Reconcile
resources and dependencies; explicitly retain, change, or supersede old commitments.
Ask participants to explain their next action and remaining conflict. Record the next
check. **Observe:** a usable shared plan. **Switch:** reconcile contradictory authority
first; L05 for open alternatives; L07 for a completed episode. This adaptation does
not replace incident command, technical procedures, or required approvals.

## L07 — Debrief a bounded event

**Opening:** “What did we expect, what happened, and what should we keep or change?”
Agree the event and how the discussion will be used. Compare records and firsthand
accounts with expectations; consider multiple causes. Select one or two changes with
owners/checks and retain what worked. **Observe:** later effects, not the meeting itself.
**Stop:** stabilize an incident first and preserve formal reporting/investigation.
Do not promise confidentiality you cannot provide or conduct an undeclared disciplinary
hearing. Retain dissent and missing accounts.

## L08 — Plan–Do–Study–Act

**Opening:** “What do we predict, and what is the smallest authorized test that could teach us?”
Plan a prediction, comparator, observations, and stop condition. Do the bounded test,
recording deviations. Study actual effects against the prediction. Adapt and retest,
adopt locally with stated limits, or abandon. **Observe:** learning across linked cycles,
not activity alone. **Stop/switch:** no experimental removal of mandatory protections;
clarify a vague problem first; L09 for capacity and L04 for competing outcomes.

## L09 — Make a capacity tradeoff

**Opening:** “Adding this needs a change in date, scope, or support. Which outcome takes priority?”
List commitments, minimum outcomes, dates, and realistic delivery/review/support effort.
Compare keep, change, defer, transfer with resources, or decline. Seek the authorized
choice and identify who must be informed. **Observe:** actual hours, quality, and work
that moved; use ranges when estimates are uncertain. **Stop:** do not unilaterally
cancel another owner's commitment or delay urgent protective action.

## L10 — Influence through evidence and genuine consultation

**Opening:** “Here is the result I think this could help. What would make it useful from your perspective?”
Identify the actual decision owner, interests, and effort; invite correction. Present
evidence, uncertainty, costs, and alternatives. Invite input that can alter the proposal,
then seek a proportionate next commitment and read back agreed, declined, or unresolved.
**Observe:** a better decision or feasible commitment. **Stop/switch:** state the open
parts of a fixed decision honestly; refusal does not authorize more pressure or isolation.
Use L09 for capacity, L04 for conflicting objectives, or a protected route for retaliation.

## L11 — Check perspectives and evidence

**Opening:** “What do we know firsthand, what are we inferring, and whose account is missing?”
Name the decision and what remains open. Obtain relevant firsthand accounts independently
or privately when useful. Separate observations, interpretations, and unknowns; examine
competing explanations including your own contribution. Retain dissent and what would
change the framing; choose the next inquiry or action. **Observe:** corrected understanding,
not a story of imagined motives. **Switch:** retrieve a single missing record directly;
L04 for conflicting interests; protected processes for sensitive allegations.

## L12 — Choose a strategic focus

**Opening:** “Which result matters over this horizon, and what could stop or wait to pursue it?”
Name outcome, horizon, decision owner, and protected commitments; retrieve missing strategic
direction. Compare credible options including current work by contribution, evidence,
capacity, dependencies, and displacement. Challenge the assumption most likely to reverse
the preference. Record choice or recommendation, conditions, tradeoff, next authorized
action, and evidence that would reopen it. **Observe:** actual resource movement, results,
and effects on displaced work. **Switch:** L09 for overload with fixed goals; implementation
for a fixed strategy; L04/owner decision for incompatible objectives; L11 for missing facts.
This local method does not create a mandate or authorize cancellation of others' work.
