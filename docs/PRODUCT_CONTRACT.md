# Product contract

## Job

Help a user form a more accurate, revisable understanding of a personal-growth
situation and convert that understanding into a chosen, testable next step.

## Distinctive behavior

The copilot earns tailoring through dialogue. It reflects, asks one material
question, updates a visible model, and stops asking when more context will not
change the decision. It can give direct advice when asked. It is allowed to use
transparent user-scored check-ins and a rich user-owned dossier; neither may
become hidden psychological assessment.

Every retained evidence item has origin/source, context, captured time, review
point, and active/superseded/rejected state; hypotheses have linked evidence,
alternatives, updated/review times, and status. The semantic validator requires
review points for active goals and experiments and for live decisions. Other
collections retain their narrower schema-defined provenance rather than
claiming one universal field tuple. A score has a user-approved construct,
bounds, anchors, purpose, decision link, and context; only the user supplies its
value. A decision record separates options, evidence, assumptions, unknowns,
model recommendation, and the user's actual choice.

## Product boundaries

- `personal-growth-copilot`: internal change system—values, goals, patterns,
  skills, experiments, review, continuity.
- `interpersonal-strategist`: concrete interpersonal situations—relationships,
  conflict, communication, boundaries, negotiation, and interaction choices.
- `goutoujunshi`: separate strategic/advisory product boundary.

Overlap is acceptable because these are separate products. The personal-growth
copilot may examine the user's contribution to a relationship pattern; it
should hand a concrete interaction strategy to the interpersonal product when
that specialist is explicitly invoked or available.

## Invocation and distribution

- explicit invocation only;
- not registered in implicit routing;
- versioned research alpha with explicit-invocation plugin distribution;
- ordinary topic practice supported through the offline learning reference player;
- no production, clinical, or outcome-efficacy claim;
- package availability does not imply production qualification or sensitive-state approval.

These are immutable release invariants: implicit invocation stays disabled;
synthetic, authored, holdout, privacy, pilot, and independent-review gates stay
separate; no average score waives a safety, privacy, consent, dependence, or
fabricated-resource failure; only the owner may record promotion after every
applicable gate points to exact evidence.

## User rights

The user can decline inquiry, challenge, scoring, dossiers, memory, or a
proposed experiment; inspect and correct any retained record; revoke an
unconsumed grant; request export or deletion through the named host; and stop
without guilt or degraded service. Host results, not conversational assurances,
are authoritative for persistence and deletion.

## Success criteria

A successful session leaves the user with:

1. a corrected or more useful working model;
2. a clearer choice or explicit uncertainty;
3. an action, experiment, or intentional decision not to act;
4. evidence and a review point;
5. less dependence on the copilot, not more.

## Non-goals

- therapy, diagnosis, treatment, crisis service, or professional determination;
- automatic emotional activation or companion behavior;
- engagement optimization, streaks, or conversation-length goals;
- hidden scoring, universal life rankings, or unvalidated assessment;
- raw journal ingestion, third-party dossiers, or emotion-salience memory;
- autonomous reminders, connectors, external writes, or background work.

## Topic-learning extension

The optional learning lane helps a user understand, practise, and apply a bounded
source-grounded topic. The first pack teaches verifiable requirements. Its supported
reference host is an offline browser player; source content and learner records are
separate. Record first attempts, assistance, writing self-review, and delayed review
without inferring mastery. See `LEARNING_RELEASE.md` and `LEARNING_PILOT.md`.
